Customer testimonial card with amber stars, italic quote, and auto-initials avatar in a brand color.

```jsx
<TestimonialCard
  quote="Map custom dengan logo sekolah kami hasilnya sangat rapi."
  name="Bu Rahma Susanti"
  role="Kepala Sekolah, SDN 04 Bekasi"
  avatarColor={1}
/>
```

Use rows of 3; increment avatarColor per card. Names use Indonesian honorifics (Bu/Pak).
